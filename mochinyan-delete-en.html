Mochinyan — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and data for Mochinyan Japanese – JLPT Words & Kanji (Android · com.pawblesse.mochinyan, iOS · Mochinyan). The developer is NyanNyanVoca (devplaynew).

Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

If you can still open the app, this is the fastest way and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open the Mochinyan app.
  2. Tap the MY tab at the bottom.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete.

Everything listed under “Data that is deleted” is removed immediately. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you already uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email us. Including the Google account address you signed up with helps us verify your request faster.

✉︎ nyannyanvoca@icloud.com

We process requests and reply within 7 business days.
