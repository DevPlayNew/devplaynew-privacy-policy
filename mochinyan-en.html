FAQ

Why does the app ask me to sign in with Apple?

Sign-in keeps your learning progress tied to you. The app stores only the anonymous user identifier issued by Apple and your name (if you choose to share it), on your device (in the Keychain). Nothing is sent to any external server, and your email address is never stored.

Where is my learning data stored?

On your device, and synced to your own Apple ID's iCloud (CloudKit private database). No one — including the developer — can access it, and you can continue learning on other devices signed in to the same Apple ID.

What can I use for free?

One full study loop per day is free; some stages and content are premium. Premium is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. All payments and refunds are handled by the Apple App Store.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Cancel in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. On a new device, tap "Restore Purchases" on the premium screen.

Will I get unwanted notifications?

No. All notifications are local (scheduled on your device) and only work if you turn them on: a daily study reminder at a time you choose, a streak reminder, and a JLPT exam D-day countdown if you set an exam date. You can turn them off anytime in MY → Notifications.

How is pronunciation audio played?

Via audio files bundled with the app and on-device iOS speech synthesis (TTS). The app does not record your voice or send audio anywhere.

I want to delete my account and data

Go to MY → Delete Account. Your learning data on the device and in iCloud is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it separately in iOS Settings → Subscriptions. You can also request deletion by email; we process requests within 7 days.

Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect and store

  • Apple sign-in data — the anonymous user identifier issued by Apple, and your name (if you choose to share it, for display only). Stored only on your device (Keychain); never transmitted externally. Your email address is not stored.
  • Nickname — if you enter one during onboarding, it is stored on your device.
  • Learning data — study progress, answer records, review schedules, and settings. Stored on your device and in your own iCloud (CloudKit private database). We operate no server of our own and cannot read this data.

Beyond this, the app collects no personally identifying information (name, email, phone number), and uses no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Apple services used

3. Third parties

We do not sell or share stored information with third parties.

4. Data storage

Learning data lives in Apple CloudKit (private database) and iCloud key-value storage, governed by Apple's privacy policy. It is accessible only through your own Apple ID.

5. Notifications

Only local notifications (scheduled on-device) are used; no remote push (APNs). The daily reminder, streak reminder, and JLPT D-day countdown all work only if you enable them.

6. Data & account deletion

Delete instantly in the app via MY → Delete Account (removes both on-device and iCloud-synced data). Uninstalling the app deletes local data, but iCloud data may remain — use in-app account deletion for complete removal. You can also request deletion by email; we process requests within 7 days. Subscriptions must be cancelled separately in iOS Settings → Subscriptions.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. When changes occur, we'll post a notice in the app and on this page.
