FAQ

How are today's cards chosen?

MochiNyan reads your surrounding place categories (cafés, stations, convenience stores, restaurants, etc.), the current weather, and time of day to prepare a card pack of 10 phrases that fit the moment. Even at the same location, the next pack can differ when the time or weather changes.

I denied location permission. Can I still use the app?

Yes. Without location, you'll get a generic time-of-day card pack instead. You can change permission anytime in iOS Settings → MochiNyan → Location.

Daily Challenge isn't showing up

Daily Challenge unlocks when you've marked 5 or more words as "I know it" in today's cards. It can be played once per day — after you complete it, the entry appears as "Done" until the next day.

My collection isn't filling up

For each word, getting 3 questions right in Daily Challenge unlocks that card's image in your collection. Since one question per word appears each day, cards fill up gradually over multiple days.

Background listening sounds unnatural

MochiNyan uses iOS on-device TTS (AVSpeechSynthesizer). Installing a high-quality Japanese voice improves the sound significantly. In iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Japanese, download an "Enhanced" or "Premium" voice.

Do I need to be signed in to iCloud?

Yes. MochiNyan syncs card content and collection progress through Apple iCloud (CloudKit). You must be signed in to iCloud in iOS Settings for the app to work properly.

Are notifications forced?

No. A gentle reminder arrives at most once per day, only at a time you set yourself. You can turn it off anytime in MY tab → Notification Settings.

I want to delete my data

Email us with the subject "Data deletion request" and we'll process it within 7 days. All your data — collection progress, bookmarks, learning records — will be permanently deleted.

Privacy Policy

1. Personal Information We Collect

MochiNyan does not collect any personal information. The app uses only an anonymous identifier through your Apple iCloud account. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. Location Data Usage

With your consent, MochiNyan detects nearby place categories (café, station, convenience store, restaurant, etc.) to recommend context-appropriate cards. We do not store or transmit your precise coordinates, place names, or movement history. You can revoke location permission anytime in iOS Settings.

3. Weather Data

Current location-based weather is queried via Apple WeatherKit to inform card recommendations. Weather data is not stored, and your location is handled according to Apple WeatherKit's policies.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share collected information with any third parties.

6. Data Storage

Your learning records (confused words, learned words, bookmarks) and collection progress are stored in Apple CloudKit's Private Database, governed by Apple's privacy policies. Only you, through your Apple ID, can access this data.

7. Notifications

MochiNyan uses only local notifications (scheduled on-device). Remote push (APNs) is not used. Notifications work only when you've explicitly enabled them and set a time, with a maximum of one notification per day.

8. Data Deletion

You can request data deletion via the email above. We process requests within 7 days. Uninstalling the app immediately deletes local data (SwiftData); iCloud data is handled according to Apple ID policies.

9. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated. When changes occur, we'll post a notice in the app and on this page.
